Shared embossed dialogs, hover transitions, selection indicators, status spinners and guide-film cues are catalogued by source owner. The audit used modal keyboard dismissal and real navigation. It did not exercise every individual decorative hover animation on real touch hardware, every Safari compositor, or measure prolonged background GPU usage across the entire site. Keep the independent GPU experiment frozen for launch. The frequent performance win in this product is knowing when a scene can be still.
