### Control and state review

Wine primary controls consistently communicate commitment. Secondary outline and text actions differ in prominence between navigation, libraries and dialogs. The embossed creation dialogs have visible depth, but their stronger shell does not repair low contrast in the surrounding text. The homepage Create dialog held keyboard focus and restored the opener on Escape. Menu entries route to four genuine destinations. Photo disclosure and the home banner warrant priority over decorative polish.

Inputs and selects in the sign-in screens have visible text labels. Editor sliders and numeric fields have a more technical density appropriate to precise work; phone touch target and virtual keyboard testing on physical devices remains open. Error and retry states were observed by withholding IndexedDB in a clean profile. The library gave a clear retry instruction, preserved the example and recovered on reload. A successful cloud share and explicit revocation were observed separately. Disabled providers, simulated timing, permission errors and editable example content should remain distinguishable.

A visual variation alone is insufficient grounds for a defect. Keep the gold letters, wine accents, botanical art, paper layers and quiet reader. Address contrasts and navigation continuity where they affect comprehension or control. [Issue register](10-MASTER-ISSUE-REGISTER.md).
