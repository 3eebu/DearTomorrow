# Dear Tomorrow — Phase 1 product spec

## Product

Dear Tomorrow is a private digital world for two people to create, schedule, share, and preserve meaningful letters, memory books, milestones, and journeys. Its first impression should feel like an editorial heirloom: warm, tactile, intimate, and calm.

## Phase 1 visitor journey

1. Arrive at a cinematic editorial home page and understand the promise: “Some words are worth waiting for.”
2. Open a fictional letter preview without creating an account.
3. Open the Create gallery and go directly to a letter, Memory Book, Journey Letters, or Our Dates demo.
4. Rename the demo book, change its cover, turn readable pages, and optionally place a local photo.
5. Explore the same fictional moments in a branching timeline or calendar; add a sample milestone to the current session.
6. Learn that the preview is not a storage, delivery, account, or offline service.

## Phase 1 features

- Responsive public narrative with four or more animated scenes.
- Keyboard-operable letter open/reveal with reduced-motion fallback.
- Create action gallery; every enabled action navigates or changes demo state.
- Memory Book with freeform title, cover palette, four pages, physical layered artwork, touch-friendly page controls, and session-only local photo preview.
- Our Dates timeline and month calendar backed by the same local state; expandable milestone details and sample add form.
- Six Journey Letters checkpoints with a moving decorative route and honest online-only preview disclosure.
- No real authentication, partner invitation, letter delivery, cloud save, public sharing, or scheduled unlock.

## Product guardrails

- Never expose fictional demo content as a real couple's data or a functioning account.
- Never include sealed private text in a real recipient response before its release; this prototype has no private-letter API or database.
- Do not claim offline delivery, live travel tracking, end-to-end encryption, public testimonials, or legal name clearance.
- The screenshots are mood and structure references only; do not copy their watermark, brand, photographs, layout, or text.
